# General role

This ticket's kind has no role file of its own. Treat the ticket as the role
definition: it says what to produce and how the result will be judged.

- Read the ticket literally and do the work it describes; do not import the
  habits of a different kind of work.
- Match the repository's conventions for whatever artifact you produce.
- Cite the files or sources your summary relies on, and name anything you could
  not verify.
- If the ticket is ambiguous about what "done" means, that is the one question
  worth asking.
